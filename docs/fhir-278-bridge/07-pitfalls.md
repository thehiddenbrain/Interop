# 07 – Pitfalls to Avoid

Severity: **S1** breaks go-live or compliance; **S2** causes data loss, rework or wrong decisions;
**S3** friction. "Mitigation" is what the bridge design does about it.

## A. Scope and strategy

| # | Pitfall | Sev | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Building against a system that is being replaced.** Point32Health is migrating both provider portals to Availity Essentials (rollout late 2026 → 2027) and has partnered with Availity + Onyx on a CMS-0057 platform. If HPHC commercial UM also moves (e.g. to MHK, which Tufts uses), a bridge to eCare has a short life. | S1 | Phase 0 confirmation with Point32Health architecture: eCare roadmap through 2028; whether Availity's "not ready" answer is about *their* PAS→278 bridge only. Design the bridge with a pluggable EDI channel so the eCare endpoint can change without touching the mapping. |
| A2 | **Assuming CMS-0057-F forces the commercial line.** Only the NH federally-facilitated-exchange QHP block of HPHC commercial is legally in scope; MA and ME exchanges are state-based; group business is out of scope. | S2 | State the business driver explicitly (parity, MA DOI pressure, provider demand). It changes priorities but not the design. |
| A3 | **Delegated UM vendors.** High-tech imaging, cardiac, MSK/spine, sleep (Evolent), genetic testing (Carelon), some behavioral health (phone-only since 2025) are *not* adjudicated in eCare. A PAS request for those must be routed elsewhere or answered "contact payer" (HCR CT), otherwise eCare rejects or mis-adjudicates. | S1 | CRD should already tell the EHR where to go; the bridge still needs a routing table by service code/category → {eCare, vendor, not-supported} and must return a clean ClaimResponse (CT + processNote with vendor contact) rather than an error. |
| A4 | **Notification ≠ authorization.** HPHC runs three distinct processes (referral, notification, prior authorization) with different timing rules (elective admission notification ≥ 1 week before; urgent/emergent within 2 business days; BH acute within 72 h in MA). PAS models an authorization decision; a notification acknowledgment has no "approved" semantics. | S1 | Decide with the business how eCare represents a notification over 278 (likely UM01 = AR with a notification flag or a specific service type, returning A1 or NA) and how the ClaimResponse expresses "notification accepted, no medical necessity decision" (NA + processNote). |
| A5 | **MA 211 CMR 52.00 (effective 5 June 2026) eliminated PA for whole categories** (primary care, preventive, maternity, outpatient SUD, PT/OT, post-cancer imaging, certain chronic-condition drugs) and imposed 24-hour urgent turnaround. Sending those through eCare produces "no auth needed" or worse. | S2 | CRD is the right place to say "no PA required"; the bridge must map eCare's "not required" outcome to NA and never to A3. Keep a state-aware rules layer. |
| A6 | **X12 IP.** Code lists and the TR3 (and the X12/HL7 crosswalk) are licensed. Coding the tables from memory or from third-party blogs will produce wrong codes and a compliance exposure. | S1 | Buy the X12 Glass subscription for 005010X217, X215 (and 006020X316 if 275 is used) before development. |

## B. Structural mapping

| # | Pitfall | Sev | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **No FHIR source for the envelope and control numbers** (ISA/GS/ST/BHT03/HL). Duplicate or non-monotonic control numbers cause TA1/999 rejections. | S1 | Durable control-number store per trading partner and per environment (test/prod); never reuse ISA13 after restart. |
| B2 | **HL hierarchy errors.** Subscriber vs dependent placement (2000C vs 2000D) depends on `Coverage.relationship` *and* on HPHC's member-ID policy. Wrong choice → every child HL02 pointer is wrong → 999 reject or AAA 64/72. | S1 | Confirm HPHC dependent handling (individual member IDs?) in phase 0; validate HL tree before send. |
| B3 | **Must-support ≠ required.** X12-required elements (UM03 at 2000E for HS/SC, UM06 for AR, DTP*435 for admissions, HI diagnosis, member ID) may be optional or absent in the PAS bundle. | S1 | Pre-flight validator with defaults where the business allows (e.g. UM06 = E) and OperationOutcome otherwise; document every default. |
| B4 | **Only the first N repetitions travel**: 12 diagnoses, 14 claim-level care team, 10 item-level care team, TR3 loop limits (service lines), 1 MSG at 2000E request. Silent truncation changes clinical meaning. | S2 | Reject over-limit bundles with an explicit OperationOutcome rather than truncating clinical content; truncate only free text and log it. |
| B5 | **Patient event vs service line modelling.** PAS puts every service in `Claim.item`; X12 has event-level (2000E) and line-level (2000F) UM/DTP/HSD. A naive mapper duplicates or contradicts values between levels (e.g. 2000E UM01 = SC but 2000F UM01 = HS is invalid). | S1 | Deterministic derivation rules (04 §4.7/4.8); reject mixed request categories. |
| B6 | **SV1 and SV2 on one authorization** – forbidden by payer practice (and probably by HPHC). Mixed professional/institutional items in one Claim cannot be one 278. | S2 | Reject at intake (400) or split into two 278s + two ClaimResponses with a business decision on which the EHR sees. |
| B7 | **Item correlation.** X12 does not carry `item.sequence`; eCare may return fewer, more or re-ordered lines, or only a 2000E-level HCR. | S1 | Encode sequence in 2000F TRN02; keep a mapping table; always emit every request item in the ClaimResponse (spec-34); addItem for extras. |
| B8 | **A6 Modified semantics.** Payers differ (deny + add vs modify in place). PAS mandates item = A6 + `addItem` with the authorized detail. HPHC returns A6 ("service approved, but with changes"). | S2 | Implement the item/addItem pattern; test with the HPHC Edit sample. |
| B9 | **Dates.** FHIR `Period` for a stay must become two D8 DTPs (435/096), never one RD8; partial dates are illegal (PAS FullDateRule); time zones (BHT04/05, ISA09/10) are the sender's local time. | S2 | Date normaliser; reject partial dates. |
| B10 | **Units and quantities.** HSD01/SV103/SV204 units are a small closed list (DA, UN, MJ, VS, HS, MN, DY, FL). UCUM units from the EHR (`{visit}`, `min`, `d`) need mapping; a missing HSD may mean "unlimited" to eCare. | S2 | Unit map + reject unknown; make HSD required when quantity present. |
| B11 | **Code systems.** SNOMED conditions (US Core) vs ICD-10-CM (HI); CPT vs HCPCS (both `HC`); NDC (`N4`) may be unsupported by eCare; POS vs type-of-bill (UM04-2 B vs A); X12 service type codes (1365) needed even when the EHR only knows a CPT. | S1 | Terminology service with versioned maps, including a CPT/HCPCS → service type (UM03) default map agreed with the UM team. |
| B12 | **Character set and lengths.** UTF-8 names, accents, em-dashes, emojis in notes; X12 delimiters inside data; AN length limits (NM103 60, NM104 35, NM105 25, REF02 50, MSG 264, N301 55, N401 30). | S2 | Transliterate/strip at the boundary (reuse this repo's `FormText` ASCII folding), escape or replace delimiters, truncate free text with a warning. |
| B13 | **Attachments.** PAS DocumentReference content is inline base64; a 278 PWK only *announces* an attachment. Without a 275 (and matching PWK06 ↔ 275 TRN control numbers) documents are orphaned; PAS mandates that the whole bundle be made available to the payer. | S1 | Phase 1: store the bundle and documents in a payer-accessible repository keyed by TRN/auth number and send PWK02 = `AA`/`EL` per CG; Phase 2: 275 if eCare accepts it. Never drop documents silently. |
| B14 | **Expedited/urgent signalling.** X12 has no unambiguous "expedited PA" flag (X12 RFI 2740); UM06 = U/03 is the closest. CMS 72-hour and MA/CT 24-hour clocks depend on it. | S1 | Map `levelOfServiceType` → UM06 and also carry urgency in MSG text if the UM team wants it; agree with eCare how urgency is detected. |
| B15 | **Cancels and updates.** FHIR update = whole Claim with flags; X12 = deltas with UM02 and REF*BB/REF*NT. If eCare returned no HCR02/REF*BB on a pended request, the follow-up cannot reference it (X12 RFI 2385: use REF*NT). HPHC forbids editing some fields (cancel + new initial). | S1 | Persist every reference number eCare returns; implement the immutable-field list; decide "reject vs auto cancel+reissue". |
| B16 | **Denied requests cannot be updated** (PAS). EHRs will try. | S3 | OperationOutcome with clear text; new request path. |

## C. Runtime and operations

| # | Pitfall | Sev | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **999/TA1 are invisible to FHIR.** A 999 rejection arriving after the HTTP response leaves the EHR thinking the request is pended. | S1 | Real-time: wait for the 999 within the timeout when the channel returns it in-band; otherwise treat a late 999 reject as a new ClaimResponse (outcome = error) and notify via subscription. |
| C2 | **Timeouts.** PAS wants ≤ 15 s; HPHC says < 10 s typical, not guaranteed. A bridge that fails on timeout will produce duplicate submissions on EHR retry. | S1 | Idempotency on Bundle.identifier/BHT03; on timeout return pended (A4) with our admin reference and reconcile by inquiry or unsolicited response; ask eCare how it de-duplicates. |
| C3 | **Pended-to-final delivery.** If eCare never pushes unsolicited 278 responses and does not support X215, final decisions never reach the EHR. | S1 | Phase 0 blocker question; fallback = eCare event feed or database poll. |
| C4 | **Higher-level AAA suppresses lower loops** (RFI 2445). A mapper that reads "no items returned" as "approved" is dangerous. | S1 | outcome = error when AAA and no HCR; items without reviewAction. |
| C5 | **Same authorization touched by two channels** (HPHConnect/fax staff vs FHIR). The bridge's copy goes stale. | S2 | eCare is the system of record; inquiry always goes to eCare when possible; expose "last synced" in ClaimResponse.meta. |
| C6 | **Provider roster requirement.** HPHC's EDI enrollment ties a submitter ID to a list of provider NPIs/TINs that must match its records. A PAS endpoint open to any contracted provider cannot pre-enumerate them. | S1 | Ask EDI for an "all contracted providers" submitter setup or an internal channel to eCare that bypasses the provider roster check. |
| C7 | **Test vs production data.** HPHC test environment, ISA15 = T, test members; PAS test bundles (Inferno) use fictional payers. | S2 | Build a fixture set from the HPHC guide samples with HPHC test members; certification checklist in `09-test-strategy.md`. |
| C8 | **Audit and reporting.** CMS-0057-F public metrics (approval/denial rates, time to decision) and PAS §11 metrics require every request to be traceable end to end. | S2 | Correlation store + metrics model from day one. |
| C9 | **PHI in logs and in error paths.** `errorPath` and diagnostics can leak member data into logs and into OperationOutcome text seen by other providers. | S2 | Redact values in logs; return paths, not values. |
| C10 | **Version drift.** PAS 2.0.1 vs 2.1 vs 2.2.1 (sequence-number guidance, DocumentInformation extension, subscription filter changes); Onyx's supported version must match what the EHRs send; HPHC guide v1.2 may be superseded. | S3 | Pin versions in config; separate endpoint per PAS version (conf-1). |

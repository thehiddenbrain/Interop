# Medical Policy Platform: assessment and design

A comprehensive assessment and first design for an enterprise medical policy database for Point32Health (Harvard Pilgrim Health Care and Tufts Health Plan): authoring, committee workflow, versioning, code mapping, prior-authorization requirement determination for every line of business, machine-readable criteria for electronic prior authorization (CMS-0057-F, Da Vinci CRD/DTR/PAS), extracts to the UM system and claims edits, and publication to the public websites and portals. It includes the vendor landscape and an evidence-based assessment of Itiliti Health, the regulatory inventory, a MoSCoW requirements catalogue, the solution design, the technology position and the build/buy roadmap with an evaluation kit.

Prepared 20 September 2026. Analysis only; no code.
